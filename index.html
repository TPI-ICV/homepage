Section 1 · Background & Mission

第1部分 · 背景与研讨会定位

Symposium Overview & Research Scope 研讨会概述与学术技术范畴

The 2026 Joint Symposium on Foundation AI, Large Language Models, and Trusted Vehicular Interaction (TPI-ICV 2026) is an internal academic exchange and technical milestone symposium co-hosted by the National University of Singapore (Chongqing) Research Institute (NUSRI-CQ) and the Chongqing Key Laboratory of Trusted Perception and Interaction Technology for Intelligent and Connected Vehicles. 2026年大模型与智能网联汽车可信感知交互联合学术研讨会暨成果海报展 (TPI-ICV 2026) 是由 重庆新国大研究院 (NUSRI-CQ) 与 智能网联汽车可信感知交互技术重庆市重点实验室 联合主办的内部高水平学术交流与技术里程碑检阅盛会。

With the rapid emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs), multimodal foundation models, and embodied intelligence, next-generation intelligent vehicles demand breakthroughs in adversarial robustness, mechanistic interpretability, safety alignment, ultra-long context reasoning, and real-time edge-cloud coordination. 伴随大语言模型 (LLMs)、多模态大基模型与具身智能技术的迅猛演进，新一代智能网联汽车正面临从感知智能向认知智能跃迁的核心挑战。亟需在车端轻量化与端云协同、对抗鲁棒性、机理可解释性、安全对齐、超长上下文推理及实时车端协同部署等核心领域实现关键技术突破。

TPI-ICV 2026 brings together laboratory PIs, research fellows (RFs), postgraduate students, and Changan Automobile R&D engineers for a one-day intensive symposium. The event features Keynote Addresses from invited scholars, Plenary Technical Sessions, an Interactive Academic Poster Session, and Live Prototype Demonstrations. 本次研讨会汇聚重点实验室各方向学术带头人 (PIs)、博士后研究员 (RFs)、博硕士研究生以及长安汽车前瞻院一线研发技术骨干，展开全天候密集式研讨。会议设置顶尖学者特邀主题报告、阶段性重大攻关汇报、成果学术海报面对面展评与实物交互系统现场Demo实测。

Section 3 · Core Research Thrusts

第3部分 · 研讨会核心研究方向

T1
Theme 1 · Core Thrust 主题一 · 核心攻关

Large Language Models (LLMs) & Foundation AI Frontiers

大语言模型 (LLMs) 与通用基础AI前沿

LLM Architecture & Efficient Inference 大模型架构与车端高效推理 Ultra-long context modeling, context compression/pruning, and low-latency inference on onboard computing units. 超长上下文建模、注意力机制剪枝与上下文无损压缩、车载受限算力平台的低时延量化加速推理。
Autonomous Agent Systems & Tool Evolution 智能体系统与工具自演进机制 Multi-agent interaction safety, tool invocation reliability, and self-evolving agent architectures. 多智能体协同交互安全、复杂环境下的外部API工具可靠调用及智能体自反思自进化机制。
Industrial & Vehicular Time-Series Foundation Models 工业与车载时序基础大模型 Multi-sensor time-series pre-training for vehicle health monitoring, predictive maintenance, and fault diagnosis. 针对多传感器高频高维时序数据的自监督预训练、整车全生命周期健康监测与动力底盘故障先兆诊断。
Edge-Cloud Collaborative LLMs 端云协同大模型分布式部署 Asymmetric deployment strategies coupling lightweight vehicle-side models with cloud-scale foundation models. 非对称端云部署策略、车端极轻量化小模型与云端超千亿基模型的无缝任务分流与知识迁移。
T2
Theme 2 · Core Thrust 主题二 · 核心攻关

Trusted AI, Safety Alignment & Mechanistic Interpretability

可信AI、安全对齐与机理可解释性

Mechanistic Interpretability & Formal Methods 机理可解释性与形式化验证 Circuit analysis, internal representation tracing, and formal verification for high-stakes vehicle AI systems. 神经网络神经元回路机制剖析、车端高风险决策内部表征追踪及安全关键控制的形式化验证。
Multimodal Adversarial Robustness 多模态对抗攻防鲁棒性 Attack and defense dynamics across LLMs, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and cockpit acoustic/speech channels. 覆盖LLM、视觉-语言大模型 (VLMs) 及智能座舱声学/语音信道的越狱注入对抗攻防机理。
Content Safety & Anti-Deepfake 内容安全护栏与深度伪造辨识 In-cabin model guardrails, digital persona copyright protection, and speech/video forgery detection. 智能座舱生成内容安全过滤护栏、虚拟形象数字版权水印与声音/图像/视频深度伪造鉴伪取证。
Data Privacy, Governance & Responsible AI 数据隐私治理与负责任AI Provable defense against data inversion/reconstruction on desensitized automotive datasets, machine unlearning, and compliance. 脱敏车端大模型训练数据的逆向还原可证防御、大模型机器遗忘 (Machine Unlearning) 与数据分类分级合规。
T3
Theme 3 · Core Thrust 主题三 · 核心攻关

Spatial Computing & Natural Human-Vehicle Interaction

空间计算与自然人车交互

Spatial Computing & In-Cabin AR 空间计算与座舱全景AR Moving-platform coordinate fusion, robust SLAM positioning under severe vibration/dynamic lighting, and cross-vendor AR glasses middleware. 动载载体坐标系实时解耦融合、剧烈颠簸/复杂变光照环境下的高鲁棒空间SLAM与跨生态AR眼镜协同中间件。
Affective Computing & Non-Invasive Sensing 情感计算与非侵入式生理传感 Non-invasive Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) for physiological tracking, cognitive workload modeling, and emotion recognition. 非侵入式脑机接口 (BCI) 生理信号采集、驾驶员认知负荷建模与多模态乘员情感状态感知。
Next-Generation Biometrics 下一代座舱生物识别与意图预测 In-cabin iris recognition, gaze estimation, and multimodal driver intention prediction. 车规级非配合虹膜识别、视线追踪注视点估计与乘员多模态交互意图提前预测。
T4
Theme 4 · Core Thrust 主题四 · 核心攻关

Robust Autonomous Driving & Emerging Vehicle Technologies

高鲁棒自动驾驶与未来汽车新兴技术

Robust Multi-Sensor Perception 多模态高鲁棒恶劣工况感知 Degradation-adaptive perception and trusted sensor fusion (Camera, LiDAR, Radar) under adverse operating conditions. 针对雨雾、眩光、遮挡等恶劣环境的传感器退化自适应估计与相机-激光雷达-毫米波雷达置信度融合。
Autonomous Driving Robustness Evaluation 自动驾驶系统全域鲁棒性评测 Standardized robustness testing suites, corner-case simulation pipelines, and explainability toolchains. 标准化端到端智驾系统鲁棒性压力测试集、Corner-Case长尾极端仿真工况生成与黑盒决策归因工具链。
Advanced Battery Systems & Energy Control 结构储能一体化电池与整车能量管控 Multifunctional structural load-bearing battery architectures and cross-domain energy management algorithms. 多功能结构承载储能一体化电池构型与跨域能耗智能协同调度算法。
